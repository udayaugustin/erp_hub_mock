Renewal Runs

Annual support and subscription renewals are raised by a scheduled run, not by a person. Each customer’s contract renews on its own anniversary; the run prices it from the contract, checks the master record, and either raises the invoice or holds it with a reason.

P
Peniel · Finance system · Renewal scheduler
The run is the volume stream: a few hundred small, nearly identical invoices on one evening. It is also the stream a manual process cannot keep up with — which is why it travels as a batch rather than one document at a time.
