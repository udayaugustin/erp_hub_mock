## The shape of the group

- **W. J. Towell & Co. LLC**, founded **1866** — one of the oldest merchant houses in Oman.
- Reported as **~50 companies** under the Towell Group umbrella. (Our proposal's 89 legal
  entities is a larger number — likely counts branches/registrations, or the client gave us
  their own figure. Worth confirming, but not a contradiction we need to resolve on screen.)
- Revenue reported at **~USD 592M** (ZoomInfo — unverified).
- Operations in **Oman, UAE, India** (and project history in Kuwait).

## Why this matters for Tuesday

Several of these are **joint ventures with external partners** — Jotun, Holcim, Unilever,
Wilhelmsen, Take Solutions, Future Pipe. That is exactly the governance situation the proposal's
**self-hosted utility (exception)** path exists for: a JV agreement can restrict sharing
transaction data outside the JV. Our invented self-hosted example was a
guess; the real group has a whole tier of JVs where that conversation is live.

It also means the **89 entities are not homogeneous**. A ready-mix plant, a car dealership, a
convenience-store chain and a drilling services company have different ERPs, different invoice
volumes and different month-end rhythms — which supports the three-method model rather than
undermining it.
