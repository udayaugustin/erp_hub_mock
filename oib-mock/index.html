O Oman Investment Bank E-Invoicing Hub

One fee invoice.
One bank.
Start to finish.

A five-step walkthrough in one short act. Oman Investment Bank issues an advisory and arrangement fee invoice for a capital-markets engagement in its own finance ERP. It is collected by the central Hub, built into a compliant PINT-OM document, proven correct against the government rules, transmitted through an accredited service provider (ASP), and reported to the Oman Tax Authority. The acknowledgement, Peppol reference and OTA reference land back on the original invoice. One entity, one invoice, one direction — the thinnest possible vertical slice.

What this slice shows — and doesn't

  • One legal entity (Oman Investment Bank), one outbound invoice, one direction. No inbound/AP flow, no multi-entity dashboard, no onboarding wizard, no mapping studio, no customer portal — all out of scope for this slice.
  • Oman Investment Bank itself, its regulators and its 2024 launch are real. Its finance ERP, CR, VATIN, the counterparty and every figure and reference number are invented for this demonstration. See README.md.
