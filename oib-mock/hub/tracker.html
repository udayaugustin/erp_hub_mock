Two stages write this document down. Record is stage 5, before transmission: the XML and its processing evidence are stored first, so there is evidence of what was sent whatever the ASP or the network does next. Archive is stage 9 — the long-term legal record, written once the acknowledgements are in.

Outcomes — three separate legs

The OTA does not clear or reject invoices directly. The ASP validates and may reject, and the ASP — not the Hub — reports the Tax Data Document to the OTA. Outcomes land on three separate, asynchronous legs, not one synchronous round trip.
