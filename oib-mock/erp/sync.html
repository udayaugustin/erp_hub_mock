The end of the walkthrough

The loop is closed.

  1. The fee invoice left Oracle Fusion Financials Cloud on its own, as a normal AR invoice.
  2. It was mapped, built as UBL 2.1, validated against the Oman PINT-OM rules and archived — before transmission.
  3. The Hub transmitted it to the accredited service provider, which reported it to the Oman Tax Authority and delivered it to the buyer.
  4. Its UUID, status, acknowledgement, Peppol reference and QR information are ready to be collected from the Hub and shown on this invoice — the same screen OIB's finance team already uses.

Building the ERP-side write-back connector is OIB's own team's work, not the Hub's. The Hub's delivery ends where it publishes the data.
