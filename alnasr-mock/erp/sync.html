This screen is an illustration. Writing these values back into the ERP is not included in our scope of work.
The e-invoicing system makes every field shown below available to the ERP. Reading them from the e-invoicing system, and creating or updating any record inside the ERP, is work that belongs to Al Nasr's ERP team or their ERP vendor — not to us.
  • We do not build anything inside the ERP. No app, connector, customisation or print format on the ERP side is part of the current proposal. What we deliver ends at the e-invoicing system's interface.
  • The ERP team collects the data and writes the record. They call the e-invoicing system, take the reference, the status and the QR information, and perform the insert or update on their own system, in their own release cycle.
  • Inbound supplier invoices must be created as drafts. When the e-invoicing system passes a supplier invoice to the ERP, the ERP team must create it as an unposted draft for a person to review. Nothing may be posted automatically.
  • If Al Nasr wants us to build it, it is quoted separately. Scope, effort, timeline and cost for the ERP-side work are estimated as a separate engagement and are not covered by the figures in this proposal.

Journey of this invoice

The end of the walkthrough

The loop is closed.

  1. The invoice left this ERP on its own, as a normal Sales Invoice.
  2. It was mapped, built as UBL 2.1, validated against the Oman rules and archived.
  3. The e-invoicing system transmitted it to the accredited service provider, which reported it to the Oman Tax Authority and delivered it to the buyer.
  4. Its reference, its status and its QR information are ready to be collected from the e-invoicing system, so they can be shown on the original record — the same screen the finance team already uses.

Nothing was exported by hand, and nobody had to leave the ERP.

Step 4 is the ERP team's work, not ours. Our delivery ends where the e-invoicing system publishes the data. Building the ERP side is a separately scoped, separately costed piece of work.
