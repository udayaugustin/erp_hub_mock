# Discovery questions for MEC

1. Which ERP raises invoices, and can it expose them by API, on-site agent or file transfer?
2. Which contracts are lump-sum (milestone) and which reimbursable (manhour)? Any unit-rate?
3. When does the client certify a milestone — before or after the invoice is raised?
4. Timesheet cut-off each month, and does the client approve hours before the invoice is posted?
5. How are retention and advance payments shown on invoices today?
6. Which clients sit outside Oman, and is any engineering used for assets in Oman?
7. Are the branches abroad registered entities or branches of the same LLC? How are recharges booked?
8. Which services are bought from abroad (software, studies), and how is reverse-charge VAT recorded today?
9. Do subcontractors already send e-invoices, and is a PO number always quoted?
10. Are disputed hours handled by credit note or by re-issuing the invoice?
11. Are the advance and retention terms standard across lump-sum contracts, and is the retention rate the same on every contract?
12. Do any clients outside Oman receive engineering for an asset located in Oman?
