# Presenter script — Vodafone Oman × Fawtara

~10 minutes. The walkthrough follows the meeting agenda. Open `index.html` — the
**scenario matrix** is the agenda on one screen. Use Next / ← → to move.

## Framing (30s)
"Vodafone invoices in six different ways — as a buyer and a seller, B2B and B2C, postpaid
and prepaid. Every one has to reach the OTA correctly. Fawtara is the accredited provider
that does it, through one pipe. Two of the six carry tax questions the OTA hasn't settled —
we come to those with a recommended position, not a shrug."

## Act I — The pipe, and the whole picture
1. **Sign in** — the console the Vodafone e-invoicing team uses.
2. **Dashboard** — all six streams on one screen: volumes, health, and the two streams
   flagged "to confirm with OTA". This is the map for the rest of the session.

## Act II — Vodafone as seller, B2B (the canonical flow)
3. **A postpaid B2B invoice** in your billing system — posted as usual. Point at the
   tracked invoice **VOD-INV-2026-00417**.
4. **Validated & cleared** — the nine stages; PINT-OM validation before anything is sent.
5. **The cleared e-invoice** — the UBL, seller identity (CR + VATIN), QR, proven correct.

## Act III — Vodafone as seller, B2C at telco volume
6. **Consumer billing** — hundreds of thousands of documents a day across postpaid and
   prepaid channels. The scale is the point.
7. **One batch a day** — the whole consumer day reported in a single overnight window.
   (Agenda: batch integration, once per day.) Note the assumption card.

## Act IV — The open questions (the value moment)
8. **Prepaid = single-purpose voucher** — tax at the top-up; buy-plan-from-balance adds no
   new tax point. "Fawtara's reading — to confirm with OTA."
9. **Out-of-bundle = aggregated to the cycle** — one invoice per cycle, not one per event.
   Again flagged to confirm. This is where Fawtara earns the room.

## Act V — As buyer, the record, the value
10. **Inbound & RCM** — inside Oman lands as a draft in your ERP; outside Oman is
    reverse-charged and captured. Matched to Vodafone by participant + CR.
11. **Reports** — VAT by stream, batch reconciliation, the register of open positions.
12. **Why Fawtara** — accredited ASP, Oman data residency, telco-scale batch, positions on
    the open questions.
13. **Back on the invoice** — the acknowledgement, status and QR written onto
    VOD-INV-2026-00417. The loop closes.

## If asked "is this how it actually works?"
Say plainly: Vodafone is real; the figures, counterparties and — importantly — the tax
treatments on prepaid and out-of-bundle are **illustrative and to confirm with the OTA**.
The systems are shown generically because the stack isn't confirmed yet. See
`VODAFONE-QUESTIONS.md`.
