# Presenter script — Ras Al Hamra Group

~10 minutes. Open `index.html` — the eighteen-step walkthrough is the story on one screen.
Use Next, or the ← → arrow keys.

## Framing (30s)
"Ras Al Hamra Group is a real, 100%-Omani conglomerate — oil & gas services, environmental
services, projects & products, and digital transformation. Five of the twelve companies
we're showing today are the Group's real, publicly known entities; the rest fill out the
group structure so the demo reads at real scale — we've flagged that split honestly in the
gap register. Every one of them has to reach the OTA correctly. This is the one pipe that
does it."

## Act I — It starts in their systems
1. **An invoice raised in the ERP** — a normal Enterprise ERP billing document, posted as usual.
2. **High-volume field billing** — RAH Manpower Services's site-desk and field-crew billing,
   batched to the Hub. The scale is the point: this is the Group's second invoice origin.
3. **Sign in to the Hub** — the console the Group platform team uses.

## Act II — The group, and the companies in it
4. **The whole group on one screen** — volumes, failures, which companies have gone quiet.
5. **Every company in the group** — Ras Al Hamra LLC, Middle East Consulting Engineering,
   Operation Excellence, Add Energy & Partner, Innovative Oil and Gas — real names, real
   sectors — plus the illustrative rest, each its own configuration record.
6. **One company in detail** — its ERP, its connection, its mapping progress.

## Act III — Bringing a company on
7-8. Connecting a new company and mapping its fields — configuration, not a new installation.

## Act IV — One invoice, end to end
9-10. Where the tracked invoice **RAH-SINV-2026-00841** sits, and the PINT-OM validation
   that proves it correct before anything is sent.

## Act V — The other direction, and the record
11-14. Supplier invoices arriving, routed by participant + CR; full history; reports.

## Act VI — What each company gets
15-18. Access issued per company, a company's own login, its own isolated view, and the
   acknowledgement written back onto the original ERP invoice. The loop closes.

## If asked "is this how it actually works?"
Say plainly: Ras Al Hamra Group is real, and five of the twelve companies shown are its
real, publicly known entities. The other seven, the VAT-group structure, the systems, the
volumes, and the tax positions are illustrative and to confirm with the Group. See
GAP-REGISTER.md.
