## Shape recommendation (non-binding)
Single legal entity, one VATIN, no VAT group. A consultancy of this type plausibly bills by
certified milestone and by approved manhour, corrects with credit and debit notes, bills clients
outside Oman if it works across the Gulf, and buys from subcontractors at home and from software
or study suppliers abroad. Those billing models are inferred from the service list, not
observed. Confidence: medium on the single-entity structure, low on the specific billing
streams and on the branch network.

## Gaps / assumptions the designer will need to fill
- VATIN, CR number, Peppol participant ID.
- ERP product, version and how it exposes invoices.
- All clients (counterparties must be fictional; no real Omani operator may be named),
  contracts, values, rates, hours.
- Subcontractors and foreign suppliers.
- Volumes per stream.
- Whether the foreign branches exist, and what legal form they take; so also whether recharges
  to them occur.
- Contract terms: retention, advance payments, variation handling.
- Headcount and revenue. Directories disagree; do not state either.
