## Group / entity structure

India — single source, unverified. No subsidiaries or Oman VAT Group found.

## ERP / systems signal

## Shape recommendation (non-binding)
Single entity, one VATIN, no VAT group — the IGC-style "six streams, one pipe" shape fits a
consultancy that bills by milestone and by manhour and buys from subcontractors and from abroad.
Confidence: medium (structure is clear; billing models are inferred from the service list).

## Gaps / assumptions the designer will need to fill
VATIN, CR, Peppol participant; ERP product; client names; contract values, rates and hours;
subcontractors and foreign suppliers; volumes per stream; the treatment of retention, advances,
export of services, branch recharges and imported services.
