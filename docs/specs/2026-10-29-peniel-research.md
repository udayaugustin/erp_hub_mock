## What the prospect is, in one line

An **accounting-software and IT solutions reseller / implementation partner** — Tally, QuickBooks,
Elate, ERPNext, Odoo, Sage — headquartered in **Bur Dubai, UAE**, with a **presence in Oman** and
offices listed in Qatar and Saudi Arabia. Not an end-user business with goods to sell: its revenue
is software licences, implementation projects, annual support contracts, training, hardware and
custom development.

## Assumptions — NOT facts, each needs confirming with the prospect

- **Which legal entity invoices Omani customers.** Penieltech's registered office is in Dubai and
  the Omani site brands itself "Peniel Computer". Whether Omani work is invoiced by *Peniel
  Technology LLC (Dubai)*, by an *Omani LLC* whose name and CR are not published, or by the Dubai
  entity exporting services into Oman, is **unknown**. This is the single most important discovery
  question: it decides whether Peniel files VAT in Oman at all, and whether its Omani sales are a
  domestic supply or an export.
- **Whether a Peniel Oman LLC exists and its CR/VATIN.** Not found in any source. The Oman site
  publishes no CR number and no VATIN.
- **Whether the Omani business has a Fawtara/PINT-OM obligation today** or is preparing for one.
  The site says "e-invoicing-ready", which is a sales line, not a filing statement.
- **Which accounting system Peniel runs for its own books.** It resells and implements Tally,
  QuickBooks, Elate, ERPNext, Odoo and Sage; it is not stated which one runs Peniel's own finance
  function. An IT reseller commonly runs one of its own house products.
- **Revenue, headcount split by country, licence-vs-services mix.** Not published. "14k+ clients",
  "50+ team" are marketing numbers.
- **Whether subscription/renewal billing is auto-generated** or raised manually. Not observable.
- **Whether it acts as a Peppol access point / ASP itself** or routes clients through one. The UAE
  site says its e-invoicing product "connects your accounting or ERP system with compliant invoice
  workflows" — the accredited-ASP relationship is not named.

## Group / entity structure

Not a VAT group in any published sense. The observable structure is a **single UAE-registered
company with country websites and listed contact offices in Oman, Qatar and Saudi Arabia, plus two

## ERP / systems signal

The strongest signal in this research, and it inverts the usual question. For most prospects we
ask "which ERP do you run?". Peniel's answer is presumably one or more of the products it sells.
What matters for a demo is not Peniel's own ERP but the fact that **Peniel would be the
integration partner facing many different customer ERPs** — Tally, QuickBooks, ERPNext, Odoo,
Sage, Elate — each of which needs its own connector to an Oman e-invoicing hub. That is a
different and more interesting story than "one company, one ERP, one pipe".

## Shape recommendation (non-binding)

**Single legal entity billing Omani customers, B2B, services-dominant — with a multi-system
product spine.** Concretely:

1. Base the mock on **`mec-mock`** (single entity, `origin/` + `hub/`, no `portal/`, B2B-only,
   live per-document submission, inbound side, credit/debit notes) rather than
   `vodafone-mock` (B2C batch — Peniel has almost no B2C).
2. The distinguishing spine is **Peniel's revenue streams as a software partner**, which are not
   the same as a normal trading company's:
   - new licence sales (one-off, goods/software with VAT),
   - implementation and customisation projects (milestone or timesheet billing),
   - **annual support and subscription renewals** (recurring, high count, the natural automation
     case),
   - training and consultancy days,
   - hardware sales (goods, with delivery note and e-way considerations),
   - exports to UAE/Qatar/Saudi customers billed from Oman, or Oman customers billed from Dubai
     — **direction to be confirmed**, and it is the OTA-position question for this prospect,
   - supplier side: **vendor licence invoices from Principals** (Tally, Intuit, Elate, Odoo,
     Frappe, Sage, resold through distribution) and imported services under reverse charge.
3. The Oman-specific "to confirm with OTA" positions this mock should carry:
   - **Software licence vs. service** on an e-invoice line — a licence key delivered
     electronically is a different classification from a consultancy day.
   - **Cross-border direction**: a UAE-established supplier invoicing an Omani customer, and the
     mirror case — the Omani entity charging the Dubai head office. Whether a recharge within one
     company is a supply.
   - **Recurring renewal invoices** — high volume, identical shape; a monthly batch run versus
     live per document.
   - **Principal (vendor) invoices in foreign currency** — AED, USD, INR — and the currency and
     exchange-rate fields an Oman e-invoice requires.
4. **Gap to call out in the demo**: Peniel's clients run heterogeneous ERPs. If the mock shows one
   `origin/` surface, name it as "the system being connected" and let the mapping screen carry the
   point that one hub profile serves Tally, QuickBooks, ERPNext and Odoo — that is the commercial
   hook for an implementation partner.

Confidence: **high** on the services-dominant B2B shape and on the recurring-renewal stream (it
follows directly from what the company sells). **Low** on the cross-border direction and on
whether an Omani legal entity exists — those two need a human answer before the demo is shown.

## Gaps a human must fill with the client

1. Which legal entity invoices Omani customers, and its CR and VATIN. *(blocking)*
2. Is Peniel an ASP, a Peppol access point, or a client of one? *(blocking for the ASP screen)*
3. Which system runs Peniel's own finance function.
4. Whether invoices to Omani customers come out of Oman or out of Dubai. *(blocking)*
5. Approximate monthly invoice and credit-note counts, split licence vs. implementation vs.
   renewal vs. training vs. hardware.
6. The actual vendor/distributor counterparties for licence purchases (all counterparties in the
   mock must be fictional or clearly illustrative).
7. Whether it bills in AED/USD/INR as well as OMR.
8. Names and count of subscription renewal cycles per year.
