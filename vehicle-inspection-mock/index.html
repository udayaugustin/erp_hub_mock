FleetView
Inspection & Accountability

From paper inspections to complete vehicle accountability.

An interactive prototype for a ~300-vehicle transport fleet — every screen is clickable sample data. An optional AI layer adds flagging, comparison and prioritisation, always advisory and human-verified.
