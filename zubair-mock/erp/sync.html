Scope of Work — SAP-side write-back

  • The ERP-side write-back connector is built by the entity. No ABAP development, CPI iFlow or Adobe/SmartForm print change on the SAP side is part of the current proposal. What the Hub delivers ends at its interface.
  • The SAP team collects the data and writes the record. They call the Hub interface, take the UUID, status, acknowledgement number, Peppol reference and QR information, and perform the update on this billing document on their own release cycle.
  • Inbound supplier invoices are created as drafts. When the Hub passes a supplier invoice to SAP, the ERP team creates it as an unposted parked/draft document for a person to review. Nothing is posted automatically.
  • If The Zubair Corporation wants us to build it, it is quoted separately. Scope, effort, timeline and cost for the SAP-side work are estimated as a separate engagement.

Everything on this screen uses sample data to show what the finished result would look like on the SAP record. It is not a working integration.

The end of the walkthrough

The loop is closed.

  1. The invoice left SAP S/4HANA on its own, as a normal billing document.
  2. It was mapped, built as UBL 2.1, validated against the Oman PINT-OM rules and archived.
  3. The Hub transmitted it to the accredited service provider, which reported it to the Oman Tax Authority and delivered it to the buyer.
  4. Its UUID, status, acknowledgement, Peppol reference and QR are ready to be collected from the Hub and shown on this billing document — the same screen the finance team already uses.

Step 4 is the entity’s SAP team’s work, not the Hub’s. The Hub’s delivery ends where it publishes the data. Building the SAP-side write-back connector is a separately scoped, separately costed piece of work.
