Inbound Routing

Live

Twelve legal entities share one VAT Group number — so the VAT number cannot be the address. Each entity is its own Peppol participant, and its Commercial Registration is what tells the Hub who an inbound document belongs to.

The rule

Participant → CR → entity
1 · Addressed to
A Peppol participant
The supplier's access point delivers to an endpoint under scheme 0248, e.g. 0248:1002715 — not to a VAT number.
→
2 · Matched by
Commercial Registration
The participant resolves to a single CR. The Hub looks that CR up in the entity directory — the shared VAT Group number OM1200094685 is only a field on the document.
→
3 · Delivered as
A draft in that entity's ERP
The Hub validates, archives the original, and creates a draft purchase invoice in the matched entity's own ERP for its finance team to review.
