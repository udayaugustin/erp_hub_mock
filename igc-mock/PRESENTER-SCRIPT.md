# Presenter script — Integrated Gas Company × Fawtara

~10 minutes. Open `index.html` — the **scenario matrix** is the agenda on one screen.
Use Next / ← → to move.

## Framing (30s)
"IGC buys gas from producers, pays a transporter, and sells to power, water and industry —
long-term and spot — with credit and debit notes and the odd export. Every one of those
documents has to reach the OTA correctly. Fawtara is the accredited provider that does it,
through one pipe. A few tax treatments the OTA hasn't settled — we bring a position, not a shrug."

## Act I — The pipe, and the whole picture
1. **Sign in** — the console the IGC e-invoicing team uses.
2. **Dashboard** — all six streams on one screen; the one flagged "to confirm with OTA".

## Act II — IGC as seller, long-term sales (the canonical flow)
3. **A long-term gas sales invoice** in the Enterprise ERP. Point at **IGC-INV-2026-00417**.
4. **Validated & cleared** — the nine stages; PINT-OM validation before anything is sent.
5. **The cleared e-invoice** — UBL, seller identity (CR + VATIN), QR, proven correct.

## Act III — Volume billing, spot sales, true-ups
6. **Monthly billing from metered volumes** — nominations, metered MMBtu, take-or-pay test,
   price, invoice. Note the assumption card.
7. **Spot sales and true-up notes** — a one-off spot invoice; a credit note and a debit note
   that reference the original invoice.

## Act IV — The open questions (the value moment)
8. **Export and government allocation** — 0% with a reason code; allocations only where
   there is consideration. "Fawtara's reading — to confirm with OTA."
9. **Take-or-pay shortfall** — consideration (5%) or compensation (outside VAT)?
   Again flagged to confirm.

## Act V — As buyer, the record, the value
10. **Producer and transporter invoices** — arrive over Peppol, land as drafts in the ERP.
11. **Reports** — VAT by stream, billing-run reconciliation, register of open positions.
12. **Why Fawtara** — accredited ASP, Oman data residency, volume-based billing.
13. **Back on the invoice** — acknowledgement, status and QR written onto
    IGC-INV-2026-00417. The loop closes.

## If asked "is this how it actually works?"
Say plainly: IGC's name, ownership and role are real; the ERP is not known; every figure,
counterparty and tax treatment is **illustrative and to confirm with the OTA**. See
`IGC-REAL-FACTS.md`.
