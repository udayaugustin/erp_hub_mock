# IGC — what is real, what is illustrative, what is unconfirmed

## Real (sources: igcoman.om, omanobserver.om, zawya)
- Legal name: **Integrated Gas Company SAOC** (igcoman.om). An earlier brief said SAOG;
  IGC's own site says SAOC, so SAOC is used.
- Established December 2022; 100% owned by the Ministry of Finance; not listed.
- Sole natural gas aggregator in Oman: manages allocations, assets, rights and obligations
  for the purchase, sale, import, export and transport of natural gas for the Government.
- Sells gas to gas-based projects (power, water, industry); about 130+ gas customers,
  8 gas sale contracts with suppliers, 1 transporter.
- First local spot gas sale has taken place.
- A Fahud–Sohar 42-inch pipeline is planned.
- No known subsidiaries, so the mock is single-entity with no VAT group.

## Illustrative (invented for the demo)
- CR number, VATIN (`OM1300054871`), Peppol participant ID.
- All counterparties (Al Noor Power Company, Gulf Desal Water, Sohar Metals Industrial,
  Wadi Kabir Fertiliser, Meridian Gas Trading, Al Hajar Gas Development, Wadi Sahil Energy,
  Trans-Oman Gas Pipelines) — fictional; no real Omani company is used.
- Volumes (MMBtu), prices (OMR per MMBtu), contract numbers, document numbers, dates,
  acknowledgement references, batch and billing-run IDs.
- The 130+ customer count is real; the per-day document counts are not.

## Unconfirmed (assumptions to confirm)
- IGC's ERP — shown as a generic "Enterprise ERP".
- Tax treatments: export zero-rating and evidence, allocation to government entities,
  take-or-pay shortfall VAT. All flagged "to confirm with OTA".
- Credit/debit notes as the true-up mechanism; month-end metering close.
- Oman e-invoicing shapes used: PINT-OM, Peppol scheme 0248, VATIN `OM` + 10 digits,
  OMR to 3 decimals, 5% VAT.

The CEO is deliberately not named anywhere in the mock.
