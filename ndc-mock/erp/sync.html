Scope of work — Dynamics-side write-back

  • We do not build anything inside Dynamics. No connector, extension or print format on the Dynamics side is part of the current proposal. What we deliver ends at the e-invoicing system’s interface.
  • The ERP team collects the data and writes the record. They call the e-invoicing system, take the reference, status and QR information, and perform the insert or update in Dynamics on their own release cycle.
  • Inbound supplier invoices must be created as drafts. When the e-invoicing system passes a supplier invoice to Dynamics, the ERP team creates it as an unposted draft for a person to review. Nothing is posted automatically.
  • If NDC wants us to build it, it is quoted separately. Scope, effort, timeline and cost for the Dynamics-side work are estimated as a separate engagement.

The end of the walkthrough

The loop is closed.

  1. The invoice left Dynamics on its own, as a normal customer invoice.
  2. It was mapped, built as UBL 2.1, validated against the Oman rules and archived.
  3. The e-invoicing system transmitted it to the accredited service provider, which reported it to the Oman Tax Authority and delivered it to the buyer.
